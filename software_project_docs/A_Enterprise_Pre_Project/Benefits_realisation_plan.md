## Purpose

Define how proposed NuBlox benefits will be baselined, measured, owned and reviewed through discovery, pilot, implementation and operation.

## Benefit-management principles

- A benefit requires a measurable change from a known baseline.
- Software output is not itself a business benefit.
- Each approved benefit must have an owner, measure, baseline, target, evidence source and review period.
- Negative impacts and disbenefits must be measured alongside benefits.
- Benefits shall be reforecast when scope, customer segment or implementation assumptions change materially.

## Discovery baseline

Primary customer research shall record for selected end-to-end workflows:

- applications used;
- spreadsheets/manual workarounds;
- handoffs;
- duplicate-entry points;
- decision/approval steps;
- rework/error points;
- elapsed and effort time where obtainable;
- current reporting effort;
- business impact of failures/delay.

## Pilot measurement

Pilot customers should agree a measurement plan before configuration/build of the pilot workflow. Pre-pilot baselines and post-pilot outcomes should use the same definitions.

Pilot measures should include:

- adoption and active use;
- workflow completion time;
- manual steps removed;
- data-quality exceptions;
- support burden;
- user satisfaction/qualitative feedback;
- measurable business outcome metrics relevant to the workflow.

## Benefit ownership

Benefit ownership ultimately belongs with the customer/business role able to change the operating process and realise the outcome, not solely with the software delivery team.

For NuBlox internal investment benefits, owners should be assigned across Product, Engineering, Commercial and Operations as the programme organisation is formalised.

## Review cadence

Proposed cadence:

- discovery: after each meaningful research cohort;
- pilot: baseline, midpoint where useful, pilot completion and 90-day follow-up;
- production customers: implementation close, 90 days, 6 months and annual value review;
- programme investment case: at each major funding/phase gate.

## Disbenefits to monitor

- implementation disruption;
- training burden;
- migration cost;
- temporary dual-running cost;
- reduced flexibility caused by standardisation;
- additional governance/administration overhead;
- dependence on NuBlox availability;
- customer lock-in concerns;
- increased security/privacy exposure through consolidation.

## Approval criteria

This plan can move to In Review when:

- the initial customer segment is validated;
- priority workflows are identified;
- measurable baselines are agreed;
- benefit owners are named;
- target-setting method is agreed;
- pilot measurement approach is incorporated into delivery planning.

## Purpose

Provide the decision structure for authorising the next NuBlox investment tranche. This version is a controlled decision template populated with the current programme position; it does not approve expenditure by itself.

## Decision requested

At version 0.1 the decision sought is **continued controlled discovery and definition**, not full-scale product build.

The next tranche should fund only the work necessary to:

- complete primary customer research;
- baseline the initial customer/problem segment;
- define project initiation and governance;
- produce business, user and non-functional requirements;
- evaluate solution/architecture options;
- establish a costed delivery plan;
- validate the highest technical, security, migration and adoption risks.

## Strategic rationale

NuBlox is investigating whether a coherent enterprise operating system can reduce the fragmentation between running an organisation and delivering the work through which it creates value.

The opportunity is strategically attractive if customer evidence shows that the cost and operational impact of disconnected systems, manual handoffs, weak traceability and fragmented professional/project work are material enough to justify change.

## Current evidence

Available controlled evidence includes:

- product vision and strategy;
- market/competitor analysis;
- initial customer-segment hypothesis;
- business case;
- feasibility assessment;
- risk/compliance/security controls;
- enterprise architecture principles;
- initial benefits and financial-evaluation frameworks.

Material evidence gaps remain, especially direct customer validation and costed delivery estimates.

## Investment options

### Option 1 — Stop / defer

Cease active product definition until stronger external evidence or funding conditions exist.

### Option 2 — Continue bounded discovery

Fund customer discovery, requirements, architecture options and planning sufficient to make a better-informed build decision.

### Option 3 — Begin broad implementation immediately

Commit substantial engineering investment before customer, requirements and architecture baselines are complete.

## Current recommendation

**Option 2 — Continue bounded discovery** is the current draft recommendation because it preserves strategic option value while limiting premature implementation commitment.

This recommendation requires approver confirmation before it becomes authoritative.

## Conditions of approval

Any approved discovery tranche should include:

- defined budget ceiling;
- named accountable owner;
- explicit deliverables;
- review date;
- customer-research target;
- requirements/architecture outputs;
- updated business case;
- updated risk assessment;
- stop/replan criteria.

## Stop / replan criteria

The programme should be reconsidered if evidence indicates that:

- target customers do not experience sufficient pain;
- willingness to adopt/pay is materially below assumptions;
- implementation/migration cost overwhelms plausible customer value;
- product differentiation collapses into commodity capability;
- architecture cannot achieve required security/reliability/economic characteristics;
- available funding cannot support a credible next phase;
- customer-specific variation makes a repeatable product model implausible.

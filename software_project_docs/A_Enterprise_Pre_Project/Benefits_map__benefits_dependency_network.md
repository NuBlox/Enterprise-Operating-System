## Purpose

Map how NuBlox capabilities and organisational changes are expected to lead to measurable benefits, so benefits are not treated as automatic consequences of software delivery.

## Strategic outcome

The strategic outcome under test is:

> Organisations can operate the business and deliver the work through a more coherent digital environment, reducing fragmentation while improving execution, control and management visibility.

## Dependency chain

```text
Product capabilities
→ adoption and process change
→ operational changes
→ measurable intermediate outcomes
→ business benefits
→ strategic outcome
```

## Benefit stream 1 — Reduced fragmentation

Enablers:
- coherent information model;
- integrated end-to-end workflows;
- deliberate integration with retained systems;
- reduced duplicate application boundaries.

Required organisational change:
- agreed authoritative information sources;
- process ownership;
- reduction of uncontrolled local workarounds.

Intermediate outcomes:
- fewer manual handoffs;
- less duplicate entry;
- fewer reconciliation steps;
- less application switching.

Potential benefits:
- reduced administration time;
- reduced operating cost;
- fewer errors;
- faster work completion.

## Benefit stream 2 — Better project/professional delivery

Enablers:
- work connected to client/commercial context;
- resources linked to delivery commitments;
- governed work products and decisions;
- current project/commercial information.

Intermediate outcomes:
- faster mobilisation;
- clearer ownership;
- earlier exception visibility;
- reduced rework and leakage.

Potential benefits:
- improved utilisation;
- improved project margin;
- reduced write-offs;
- improved client delivery performance.

## Benefit stream 3 — Stronger control and assurance

Enablers:
- explicit approvals/authority;
- audit/evidence capture;
- controlled records;
- security and classification controls.

Intermediate outcomes:
- clearer decision provenance;
- less retrospective evidence gathering;
- earlier control exceptions.

Potential benefits:
- reduced compliance effort;
- reduced risk exposure;
- stronger audit readiness;
- increased trust in operational data.

## Benefit stream 4 — Better management information

Enablers:
- operational information captured at source;
- common identifiers/context;
- traceable business events;
- governed metrics.

Intermediate outcomes:
- less offline reporting assembly;
- more current forecasts and status;
- improved cross-functional visibility.

Potential benefits:
- faster decisions;
- more accurate forecasting;
- earlier intervention;
- lower reporting effort.

## Benefit stream 5 — Lower change and growth friction

Enablers:
- reusable configuration;
- controlled extensibility;
- repeatable implementation/migration patterns;
- coherent integration model.

Intermediate outcomes:
- faster onboarding of teams/customers/capabilities;
- fewer bespoke forks;
- more predictable upgrades.

Potential benefits:
- lower implementation cost;
- faster time to value;
- improved product gross margin;
- higher customer retention/expansion potential.

## Critical dependencies

Benefits depend on more than product delivery. Critical dependencies include:

- user adoption;
- process ownership;
- data quality;
- migration quality;
- management sponsorship;
- training/change support;
- decommissioning or rationalising redundant systems where intended;
- measurable baselines.

## Evidence status

All benefit links in version 0.1 are hypotheses. They require customer research, pilot measurement and agreed baselines before financial valuation.

## Purpose

Maintain a controlled summary of customer-discovery evidence used to validate the NuBlox product vision, target market, buyer problems and adoption assumptions.

## Research status

**Primary customer discovery has not yet been completed to a standard sufficient for product approval.**

This document therefore records the current hypotheses and the evidence programme required to validate them. It must not be read as proof that the stated customer problems are universal or commercially sufficient.

## Current target-customer hypothesis

The initial segment to investigate is UK mid-market multidisciplinary built-environment consultancies and project-services firms, approximately 50–500 employees.

Likely participants to research include:

- managing director / chief executive;
- finance director / finance manager;
- commercial director;
- operations director;
- project director / project manager;
- discipline lead;
- resource manager;
- business development / sales lead;
- information/document management lead;
- quality, risk or compliance lead;
- professional practitioners producing deliverables;
- system administrators;
- external client or delivery partners where appropriate.

## Hypotheses requiring validation

### H1 — fragmented systems create measurable operating cost

Customers may use separate tools for finance, CRM, project management, resource planning, time recording, document management and specialist professional work, creating duplicate entry and reconciliation.

### H2 — project/professional delivery is weakly connected to enterprise operations

The actual work products and delivery decisions may be managed outside the systems that control opportunity, appointment, resources, project economics, billing and management reporting.

### H3 — handoffs are a material source of delay and risk

Email, spreadsheets and informal communication may bridge gaps between systems or teams, making ownership and current state difficult to determine.

### H4 — buyers would fund better continuity

The pain must be economically significant enough for a buyer to sponsor change rather than tolerate existing integration and manual processes.

### H5 — one repeatable operating pattern exists across multiple firms

NuBlox only has a scalable product opportunity if the core problems and required outcomes repeat across customers without excessive bespoke implementation.

## Research questions

### Business context

- What services does the organisation sell?
- How is work won, contracted, planned, delivered, reviewed, billed and closed?
- Which activities are common across projects and which vary by discipline/client?

### Current system estate

- Which systems support CRM, finance, projects, resources, time, documents, quality, HR and professional delivery?
- Which information is duplicated?
- Where are spreadsheets, email or manual trackers required?
- Which specialist tools are essential and why?

### Pain and economics

- Where does work wait?
- Where is information re-entered or reconciled?
- Which failures cause write-offs, margin erosion, delayed billing, rework, compliance risk or client dissatisfaction?
- How much time/cost is associated with those problems?

### Governance and control

- How are responsibilities, approvals, reviews and decisions managed?
- How is evidence retained?
- What must be auditable or reconstructable?

### Adoption and buying

- Who owns the problem?
- Who controls budget?
- What existing systems are contractually or operationally difficult to replace?
- What implementation time and disruption are acceptable?
- What evidence would be required to trust a new platform?

## Minimum discovery cohort

Before the initial segment is treated as validated, the programme should obtain direct evidence from multiple independent organisations, ideally including:

- at least 3 multidisciplinary consultancies of different sizes;
- at least 2 specialist/project-service firms;
- at least 1 larger organisation with mature enterprise systems for contrast;
- participants from finance/commercial, delivery and executive perspectives rather than a single role.

The exact research sample may be changed if evidence shows a different segment should be prioritised.

## Evidence capture

For every research interaction record:

- organisation profile;
- participant role;
- date and research method;
- systems used;
- workflow observed/discussed;
- pain statement;
- evidence/quantification;
- workaround;
- decision/buying context;
- requested capability/outcome;
- contradictions to existing hypotheses;
- follow-up required.

Personally identifiable or commercially sensitive research data must be handled according to applicable privacy and confidentiality requirements.

## Current evidence available

Current evidence is limited primarily to:

- official vendor positioning and product documentation;
- observed market structure;
- known category overlap between enterprise systems and project/delivery platforms;
- product hypotheses recorded in the NuBlox pre-project documents.

This is insufficient to approve the target-market or investment case on its own.

## Exit criteria

This document can move beyond Draft only when:

1. direct customer evidence has been collected;
2. material pains have been observed in more than one organisation;
3. economic or operational impact is quantified where possible;
4. buyer/sponsor roles are understood;
5. switching and implementation constraints are documented;
6. evidence either supports or causes revision of the product strategy.

## Purpose

Define how NuBlox programme expenditure will be structured, estimated, approved, monitored and reforecast before numeric budgets are formally authorised.

## Current status

Version 0.1 defines the funding model and cost-control categories only. No approved budget amount is stated because the delivery plan, resource model and implementation scope are not yet baselined.

## Funding principles

1. Funding should follow evidence gates rather than assume the complete long-term product can be costed accurately at inception.
2. Each funding tranche must have explicit outcomes, decision rights and stop/replan criteria.
3. Committed spend, forecast spend and aspirational roadmap cost must remain distinct.
4. Product engineering, customer-specific implementation and commercial acquisition costs must be visible separately.
5. Security, compliance, migration, operations and support cannot be treated as optional later-stage add-ons.

## Budget structure

### A. Product discovery and programme definition

- customer research;
- product/domain analysis;
- market/commercial research;
- programme management;
- legal/privacy/compliance advice;
- early architecture/prototyping.

### B. Product engineering

- product management;
- architecture;
- software engineering;
- UX/design;
- QA/test automation;
- data/integration engineering;
- technical documentation.

### C. Platform and operations

- cloud infrastructure;
- environments;
- CI/CD and developer tooling;
- observability;
- backup/DR;
- support/service tooling.

### D. Security and assurance

- security engineering;
- vulnerability tooling;
- independent security testing;
- compliance/certification work;
- privacy and legal review;
- audit readiness.

### E. Customer implementation

- onboarding;
- configuration;
- migration;
- integrations;
- training/change management;
- pilot support.

### F. Commercial and go-to-market

- sales;
- marketing;
- events/industry engagement;
- solution engineering;
- customer success;
- partner development.

### G. Corporate / contingency

- insurance;
- professional services;
- accounting/legal;
- contingency and management reserve.

## Funding stages

### Tranche 0 — Discovery / definition

Authorises controlled pre-project work, customer research, requirements discovery and solution-option analysis.

### Tranche 1 — Architecture / validated prototype

Authorises architecture, product design, prototypes and technical spikes sufficient to validate major risks.

### Tranche 2 — Foundation / first end-to-end slice

Authorises production-grade platform foundations and one representative end-to-end business slice.

### Tranche 3 — Initial market solution / pilots

Authorises the coherent product scope needed for controlled pilot customers.

### Tranche 4 — Production launch and scale

Authorises production readiness, operating capacity, commercial launch and subsequent expansion.

Each tranche requires an updated business case and risk position.

## Cost-estimation maturity

Budget estimates should carry a confidence/maturity classification such as:

- Conceptual;
- Indicative;
- Planned;
- Baselined;
- Committed;
- Actual.

No figure should be presented without its maturity class, assumptions and date.

## Variance control

Once baselined, reporting should distinguish:

- baseline budget;
- approved changes;
- current forecast;
- actual spend;
- committed spend;
- variance;
- contingency remaining;
- explanation and corrective action.

## Funding evidence required

Before numeric approval, the model requires:

- resource plan and rate assumptions;
- delivery roadmap and scope;
- infrastructure assumptions;
- security/compliance plan;
- customer implementation model;
- commercial staffing/acquisition assumptions;
- TCO model;
- risk-adjusted contingency;
- cash-flow timing.

## Purpose

Define the programme sequence for turning the NuBlox product hypothesis into an evidence-backed, implementable and operable enterprise software product without prematurely freezing a feature catalogue.

## Roadmap principles

- Evidence before irreversible commitment.
- Customer problem before feature list.
- Requirements before architecture.
- Architecture before large-scale implementation.
- Security, privacy, migration and operability are product concerns from the start.
- Each stage must leave a controlled evidence trail and explicit decision gate.

## Stage 0 — Product and market definition

Current stage.

Outcomes required:

- product vision and strategy;
- market and competitor analysis;
- customer-segment hypothesis;
- primary customer discovery;
- business case and feasibility;
- benefits, risks, compliance and financial model;
- enterprise architecture principles;
- initial programme governance.

Exit gate: sufficient evidence exists to initiate a formal product-development project and baseline high-level scope.

## Stage 1 — Project initiation and requirements discovery

Outcomes:

- project charter/PID;
- stakeholder register and governance;
- high-level scope and success criteria;
- business requirements;
- user/persona research;
- end-to-end business scenarios;
- functional and non-functional requirements;
- business rules;
- data/integration/reporting requirements;
- acceptance model and traceability framework.

Exit gate: the problem and required outcomes are sufficiently defined to design a solution.

## Stage 2 — Architecture and product design

Outcomes:

- solution architecture;
- system context and major bounded responsibilities;
- enterprise information/data architecture;
- security/privacy architecture;
- tenancy/deployment decision where required;
- integration strategy;
- UX/information architecture;
- migration architecture;
- ADRs for material decisions;
- implementation slices and test strategy.

Exit gate: architecture is coherent, testable and traceable to approved requirements.

## Stage 3 — Foundation implementation

Expected themes, subject to requirements and architecture:

- development/repository standards;
- environments and CI/CD;
- identity/access foundation;
- core persistence/data controls;
- audit/observability foundation;
- configuration/extensibility foundations;
- first end-to-end business slice.

Exit gate: one controlled end-to-end slice proves the architecture and delivery method.

## Stage 4 — Initial market solution

Build the coherent minimum product needed to solve a meaningful set of end-to-end outcomes for the validated initial customer segment.

This stage is not a generic MVP shortcut. Scope is determined by the smallest commercially credible operational system that can demonstrate the NuBlox value proposition.

Exit gate: pilot customers can perform agreed live workflows with measurable outcomes and controlled support.

## Stage 5 — Production readiness and launch

Outcomes:

- security and performance assurance;
- operational readiness;
- support model;
- backup/recovery and continuity;
- deployment/release controls;
- data migration and onboarding procedures;
- commercial packaging;
- training/documentation;
- pilot acceptance and launch decision.

## Stage 6 — Expansion

Potential expansion dimensions:

- additional customer sizes;
- adjacent CBE organisation types;
- deeper professional/project delivery capabilities;
- broader enterprise-operating capabilities;
- additional jurisdictions;
- additional industries where the operating model fits;
- ecosystem and partner capabilities.

Expansion must not compromise the coherence or maintainability of the core product.

## Dependencies

The roadmap depends on continuous updates to:

- customer research;
- risk register;
- business case;
- financial/TCO models;
- compliance register;
- benefits-realisation plan;
- architecture decisions;
- delivery evidence.

## Roadmap status

No delivery dates are approved in version 0.1. Dates, budgets and resource commitments require the funding model, programme charter and project-initiation baseline.

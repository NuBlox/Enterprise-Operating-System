## Purpose

Identify the stakeholder groups whose needs, authority, risk exposure or adoption materially affect the NuBlox programme and define how their involvement should influence discovery and governance.

| Stakeholder group | Primary interest | Influence | Current engagement | Required next action |

## Stakeholder segmentation

### Manage closely

- product/investment decision makers;
- target customer executive sponsors;
- target operational leaders;
- engineering/architecture leadership;
- security/privacy/compliance decision makers.

### Involve continuously

- representative end users;
- finance/commercial users;
- administrators/integration stakeholders;
- customer implementation/change leads.

### Consult at defined gates

- legal advisers;
- professional/industry bodies;
- specialist technology vendors;
- implementation partners;
- auditors/assurance specialists.

### Monitor

- broader market participants;
- future segments/jurisdictions;
- emerging regulatory and technology stakeholders.

## Key stakeholder tensions to manage

- enterprise standardisation versus professional flexibility;
- broad product ambition versus initial market focus;
- integrated platform value versus specialist-system depth;
- strong control/evidence versus low-friction user experience;
- speed to market versus security, reliability and maintainability;
- customer-specific requests versus reusable product design;
- migration ambition versus business disruption.

## Discovery representation requirement

Customer discovery should not rely on executive interviews alone. Each validated workflow should include, where applicable:

- person performing the work;
- manager/accountable owner;
- commercial/financial stakeholder;
- approver/assurance role;
- IT/data/security stakeholder;
- external participant affected by the handoff.

## Governance implication

The formal Programme Charter and later Project Charter should convert this stakeholder map into named decision authorities, RACI/accountability, communication routes and escalation mechanisms.

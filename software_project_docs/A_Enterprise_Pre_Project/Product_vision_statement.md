## Purpose

Define the intended future state for the NuBlox Enterprise Operating System before detailed requirements, architecture or implementation choices are made.

## Vision

**NuBlox will enable organisations to operate the business and deliver the work they exist to perform through one coherent digital operating environment.**

The product should allow people to understand what needs attention, perform work, create and change business information, collaborate across organisational boundaries, make and evidence decisions, manage obligations and produce reliable business outcomes without unnecessary fragmentation between disconnected systems.

## Problem statement

Organisations commonly distribute their operating reality across combinations of ERP, CRM, HCM, project systems, specialist industry tools, document repositories, spreadsheets, email and informal processes. The result can include duplicate information, broken handoffs, weak traceability, inconsistent control, manual reconciliation and poor visibility of work in progress.

NuBlox is intended to reduce that fragmentation by connecting the organisation, its work, its information and its control environment in a coherent product experience.

## Intended users

The product is expected to serve organisations with complex cross-functional and delivery work. User groups to validate include:

- people performing day-to-day operational or professional work;
- managers responsible for teams, projects, services or operations;
- executives requiring enterprise visibility and decision support;
- people responsible for finance, commercial management, risk, assurance or compliance;
- administrators responsible for configuring and operating the platform;
- external parties participating in shared business processes where appropriate.

These are discovery hypotheses, not a final role model.

## Intended outcomes

NuBlox should make it possible to:

1. understand what work is required and why;
2. perform that work with the relevant information available in context;
3. preserve continuity between upstream and downstream activities;
4. keep responsibilities, approvals, decisions and evidence connected to the work;
5. reduce duplicate entry and manual reconciliation;
6. create operational management information from the work itself;
7. support different organisations and industries without fragmenting the product into unrelated customer-specific systems;
8. coexist deliberately with specialist systems when replacing them would not create sufficient value.

## Product principles

### Business outcome before feature

The product should be designed around complete business outcomes rather than isolated screens or modules.

### Business information and work belong together

Work should remain connected to the information, transaction, record, document, model, asset or other business subject it creates or changes.

### Governance is part of normal work

Responsibility, approval, decision, evidence and assurance should be captured through the working process wherever practical rather than reconstructed later.

### Complexity should be absorbed by the product

Enterprise operations may be complicated; users should not need unnecessary knowledge of internal application boundaries to perform their work.

### Configuration must preserve coherence

NuBlox will need to support variation between organisations, industries and jurisdictions without producing disconnected product forks.

### Traceability is a first-class product characteristic

Important work should remain explainable: what happened, when, by whom, in what context, using what information and with what outcome.

## Scope boundaries at this stage

This vision does **not** decide:

- the final feature or capability catalogue;
- the final target market or launch segment;
- the data model;
- the application architecture;
- the technology stack;
- the route/navigation model;
- the commercial packaging or pricing model;
- which specialist capabilities are built natively versus integrated;
- the final regulatory or geographic scope.

Those decisions require controlled follow-on documents and evidence.

## Vision test

The vision should be considered valid only if customer and market discovery demonstrate that organisations experience material operational fragmentation and that a more coherent operating environment creates enough measurable value to justify adoption and change.

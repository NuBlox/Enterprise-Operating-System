## Purpose

Authorise and bound the initial NuBlox product-definition and software-development project under the wider Enterprise Operating System programme.

## Project objective

Define, design, build and validate the first commercially credible NuBlox product scope for the validated initial customer segment, with traceability from customer/business needs through requirements, architecture, implementation, verification and pilot outcomes.

## Business need

The project investigates and addresses the operational fragmentation that can arise when an organisation runs its enterprise operations and delivers professional/project work through disconnected applications, information stores and manual processes.

The project must prove that NuBlox can create measurable value by improving continuity across selected end-to-end business outcomes rather than by merely consolidating software features under one interface.

## Initial customer hypothesis

The current working hypothesis is UK mid-market multidisciplinary built-environment consultancies and project-services firms. This remains subject to primary customer validation and may change through controlled decision-making.

## Project scope

### Included

- customer and user research;
- business/process/workflow analysis;
- functional and non-functional requirements;
- information/data requirements;
- product and UX design;
- solution/security/data/integration/migration architecture;
- controlled software implementation;
- automated and manual verification;
- deployment/operability foundations;
- pilot onboarding/migration;
- measurement of agreed customer outcomes;
- documentation, governance and traceability.

### Excluded unless separately approved

- unrestricted coverage of every enterprise function or industry;
- customer-specific product forks;
- unsupported regulatory/jurisdictional scope;
- broad production rollout before pilot evidence;
- replacement of specialist systems where no clear customer value is demonstrated;
- implementation decisions not traceable to requirements or approved technical rationale.

## Primary deliverables

1. Approved project initiation baseline.
2. Customer/problem evidence and prioritised end-to-end scenarios.
3. Requirements baseline and traceability matrix.
4. Solution architecture and material ADRs.
5. Product/UX design baseline.
6. Production-quality foundation implementation.
7. First representative end-to-end business slices.
8. Test/security/performance evidence.
9. Pilot implementation and migration evidence.
10. Benefits/value measurement and launch recommendation.

## Success criteria

Detailed measures are controlled in `Objectives_and_success_criteria.md`. At charter level the project succeeds when it demonstrates:

- validated customer/problem fit;
- coherent end-to-end execution for agreed priority workflows;
- secure, resilient and maintainable architecture;
- acceptable usability and adoption evidence;
- repeatable implementation/migration approach;
- measurable customer value;
- commercially plausible operating economics;
- controlled path to production launch.

## Constraints

Current known constraints include:

- customer evidence is incomplete;
- budget is not yet baselined;
- no final technology stack is authorised;
- no final functional taxonomy is authorised;
- security/privacy/compliance requirements must be established before production use;
- initial delivery capacity and named governance roles remain to be confirmed.

## Governance

The project operates under the Programme Charter and shall use controlled project documents, Git history and explicit approvals for material decisions.

Project roles, RACI, governance forums, escalation and reporting will be defined by the Project Initiation Document and Governance Structure.

## High-level risks

- insufficient customer value or willingness to change;
- scope expansion beyond delivery capacity;
- premature architecture decisions;
- migration/integration complexity;
- security/compliance shortfall;
- poor product usability/adoption;
- inability to provide sufficient professional depth without bespoke forks;
- cost of implementation exceeding plausible commercial value.

## Authority granted by approval

Approval of this charter would authorise the project to proceed through requirements, architecture and controlled implementation within an approved funding envelope and governance model. It would not authorise unlimited scope or production launch.

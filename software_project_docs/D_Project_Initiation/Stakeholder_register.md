## Purpose

Maintain the project-level stakeholder baseline, including each stakeholder group's interest, influence, required engagement, decision role and current information gaps.

## Stakeholder information to capture for named participants

When individual stakeholders are appointed, the register should additionally record:

- name and organisation;
- role/title;
- contact route;
- decision authority;
- availability;
- confidentiality/access constraints;
- influence/interest assessment;
- preferred communication method;
- current support/concerns;
- actions and commitments.

## Engagement rules

- Do not treat one executive interview as sufficient evidence for a workflow.
- Include people who perform, manage, approve and consume the outcome of work.
- Include IT/security/data stakeholders before architecture decisions that affect them.
- Include external participants where the workflow crosses organisational boundaries.
- Record conflicting stakeholder needs explicitly rather than silently choosing one.

## Current gaps

The principal gap at version 0.1 is that most stakeholder categories are not yet represented by named external customer participants. Customer recruitment is therefore a prerequisite for credible requirements discovery.
